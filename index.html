How we think

A structured approach to complex matters.

Complexity does not always come from a lack of information. Sometimes, it comes from having too much information, competing considerations or no clear basis for a decision.

Frame

Understand the context, objectives, stakeholders and constraints.

02 / MAP

03 / ASSESS

04 / GUIDE

05 / ALIGN

The work

What our thinking becomes.

Good analysis should lead somewhere. Depending on the matter, Arkadia's work may take the form of:

Regulatory Assessment

An examination of applicable requirements and their implications for the business.

Governance Review

An assessment of decision-making structures, responsibilities and governance practices.

Risk Assessment

A structured view of relevant exposures, gaps and potential consequences.

Compliance Framework

A practical structure for translating requirements into policies, responsibilities and processes.

Legal Opinion

A comprehensive, reasoned opinion based on relevant facts, documents, applicable law and risk considerations.

Action & Implementation Framework

A practical outline of recommended next steps, responsibilities and priorities.

The aim is not simply to produce more documents. It is to make the work useful. Actual deliverables are defined by the context and agreed scope of each engagement.
